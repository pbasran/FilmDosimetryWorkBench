VAIDER Lab

Film Dosimetry Workbench

Calibrate radiochromic film, convert scans to dose maps, and compare them with a DICOM RTDOSE from the planning system. Everything runs in your browser; scans never leave your computer.

Launch the application → Opens in this tab. Open in a new tab · Quick guide
1

Calibrate

Click film pieces of known dose, fit the curve with blue-channel renormalization, and save the calibration (.json).

2

Dose map and export

Straighten and crop a scan, convert it to dose, and export a 32-bit TIFF (Gy) or a DICOM RTDOSE.

3

Compare

Overlay the film on an RTDOSE plane, align it with the arrows, Auto-align or Optimize shift, and read profiles, difference and gamma.

For reliable dosesScan the calibration strip, the 0 Gy background strip and the sample films together in one scan, with scanner colour corrections off.
